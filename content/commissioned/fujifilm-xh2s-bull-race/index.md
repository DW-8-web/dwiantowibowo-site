Produced as an official field showcase for the launch of the flagship **Fujifilm X-H2S**, this commercial film follows acclaimed Indonesian documentary photographer **Ulet Ifansasti** capturing the raw energy, high velocity, and mud-splattered traditions of *Pacu Jawi* (traditional bull race) and *Silek Lanyah* (mud martial art) in Tanah Datar, West Sumatra.

The production documented the camera's high-speed autofocus tracking, burst capabilities, and in-body image stabilization amid chaotic field conditions where flying mud and fast-moving subjects leave no second chances.
