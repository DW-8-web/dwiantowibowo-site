I am an independent visual storyteller with a background in newsroom photojournalism. Specializes in long-form documentary photography focusing on human rights, state-sanctioned displacement, religious intolerance, and environmental conflicts. Committed to visual advocacy and highlighting the social realities of marginalized communities across the Indonesian archipelago.
